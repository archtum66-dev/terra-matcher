    Settlement drafts (unsigned)

    Fills appear here as pairs of token_diff intents.

    Each pair nets to zero for every token. In production both parties sign their half in their own wallet and the pair is submitted to the NEAR Intents contract. Nothing is signed or sent from this page.

    Rules of the matcher: price-time priority, fills at the resting order's price, self-trade cancels the newest order, post-only never crosses. See docs/SPEC.md.
